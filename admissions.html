Common questions

Good to know.

For program-specific or current requirements, contact admissions directly.

How do I choose the right program?

Start with the subjects and problems you enjoy. Compare course topics on the courses page, then contact admissions to talk through your options.

Where can I find current entry requirements?

Requirements can vary by program and applicant background. Email admissions@hitechcollege.edu for the latest official information.

Can I visit the campus?

Contact admissions to ask about visit options and accessibility needs. You can also explore spaces and student life on the campus page.

Can I get help with my application?

Yes. The admissions team can explain the process and point you to the right support. They cannot complete application materials on your behalf.
